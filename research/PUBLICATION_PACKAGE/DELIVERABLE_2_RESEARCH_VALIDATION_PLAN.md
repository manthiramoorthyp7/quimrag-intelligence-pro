# Deliverable 2 — Research Validation Plan

Prioritized by how directly each item affects whether the paper's central claims (Deliverable 1, Q10) are defensible, not by implementation effort. "Status" reflects what is true as of this session.

| Priority | Experiment | Why it matters | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P0** | Document-overlap check for the NDSU-substitute sample (70 queries, seed 17) and the RAGAS-generation sample (60 queries, seed 23) | Both samples were built with the same un-corrected, row-level-only `split_calibration_eval()` that caused the main benchmark's leakage. If either has document overlap, their reported EAR-gating numbers (84.3%→96.0%; Faithfulness 0.514→0.619) are subject to the same inflation risk and must be flagged or re-run. Currently **unverified either way** — not confirmed clean, not confirmed leaky. | **Not done.** Highest-value remaining check; cheap to run (an `overlap_report`-style function already exists in `ohrbench_adapter_v2.py` and generalizes directly). |
| **P0** | Re-run the NDSU-substitute and/or RAGAS-generation studies on a document-disjoint split, if P0's check finds overlap | Completes the correction started this session for the main benchmark. | **Not done**, contingent on the check above. |
| **P1** | EAR signal-level ROC-AUC / Brier score / calibration-reliability diagnostics for each of the 4 signals individually | The corrected main-benchmark result (Deliverable 1, Q6 item 4) tells us the *combined* model's effect is not significant at n=173 calibration / n=247 evaluation. It does not tell us whether any *individual* signal (agreement, ocr_confidence, contradiction, complexity) is itself a reliable predictor of top-1 correctness independent of the others. This is the single highest-value next experiment for salvaging a defensible EAR-specific claim. | **Not implemented**, any split, ever. Stated as a scope boundary before the corrected re-run and still true. |
| **P1** | Repeat the corrected main-benchmark run at 2–3 additional random seeds (different stratified samples, same document-disjoint construction) | Every CI in this project, before and after correction, captures only within-split bootstrap variance, not split-to-split variance. A single seed cannot distinguish "the +6.1-point trend is real but this session's split was unlucky" from "the effect is genuinely near zero." | **Not done.** Single-seed throughout. |
| **P2** | Larger or differently-constructed calibration set for EAR | The corrected fit's 15.4% coverage is plausibly an artifact of a calibration set (173 queries, 109 documents) too small or too narrow in its OCR-confidence/agreement range to learn a well-separated decision boundary, independent of whether the underlying effect exists. Worth testing before concluding EAR has no effect at all. | **Not done.** Flagged as a hypothesis in `FINAL_RESULTS.md`, not tested. |
| **P2** | A manually-labeled contradiction-detection test set, to measure the `contradiction` signal's own precision/recall in isolation | The `contradiction` signal's fitted weight shrank substantially under correction (−0.579 → −0.033); unclear whether this reflects a real weakening of its informativeness or is a calibration-sample artifact like `ocr_confidence`'s. | **Not attempted** — would require new human annotation effort, out of scope for this session. |
| **P3** | Re-run the OCR-robustness study against `qas_v2.json` / the document-disjoint sample | Currently uses the older `qas.json`-derived degraded-document set; not implicated by the leakage finding (no calibration/eval split used) but still worth aligning to the corrected dataset family for consistency. | **Not done.** Low priority — this study's own internal design is not compromised by the leakage bug. |
| **P3** | BEIR / HotpotQA / Natural Questions as a second, genuinely independent benchmark family | Every result in this project, corrected or not, comes from OHR-Bench and its sampled subsets. A second benchmark family would be the strongest available evidence against "this is an OHR-Bench-specific artifact." | **Not attempted.** Considered and explicitly declined for this session (`research/FINAL_VALIDATION/DATASET_SELECTION.md`) given time budget; HotpotQA specifically flagged as the best match for testing the `complexity` signal. |
| **P3** | Multi-rater manual review of generation-quality failure cases (currently single-reviewer) | Strengthens the qualitative error analysis beyond one person's judgment. | **Not done.** |

## What this means for Deliverable 4 (the paper)

The paper is written around what is **already confirmed** (RVQ fix, OCR-confidence calibration, the leakage-correction case study) rather than treating the P0/P1 items above as already closed. Where EAR's own accuracy claim is discussed, it is presented as an open question with this table's P0/P1 items named as the concrete next steps — not implied to be settled.
